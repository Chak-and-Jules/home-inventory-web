## 2025-02-13 - [aria-current for Navigation Links]
**Learning:** [Using aria-current="page" on active navigation links is a simple yet highly effective way to explicitly communicate the active route context to screen reader users, avoiding reliance solely on visual cues like background colors.]
**Action:** [Always include aria-current evaluating to "page" or undefined conditionally based on route matching when building navigation sidebars and bottom bars to ensure equitable keyboard and screen reader accessibility.]
